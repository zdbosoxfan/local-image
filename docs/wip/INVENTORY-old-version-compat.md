# Inventory: compatibility with previous Local Image / LightCraft / PhotoCraft versions

Owner decision (Oct 14 2026): **v2 must not read, migrate or alias anything from earlier Local Image,
LightCraft or PhotoCraft versions.** Keep everything needed for other apps' and industry formats
(PSD, TIFF, DNG/raw, XMP incl. Adobe `crs:`, Lightroom/Capture One importers, ICC, LUTs, ABR,
swatches, Affinity…) and keep internal APIs that are simply current code.

Read-only inventory of branch `claude/v2-continue` at `2922c4a8` (worktree `integrate`). Nothing was
edited or built. Line numbers are as of that commit.

Legend for "safe to delete?":
- **yes** — exists only for old-version input; callers/tests listed move as noted.
- **care** — removal is right but touches live behaviour, a format another app also uses, or a
  safety net that also protects *current* files; the reason is given.
- **KEEP** — looks like compat but must stay (other-app format, current-file damage recovery, or
  a Photoshop/Lightroom feature that happens to be called "legacy").

Status: complete (job grouping, ownership, order and risks at the end).

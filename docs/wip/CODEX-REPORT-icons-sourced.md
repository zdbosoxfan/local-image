# Approved mixed Glossy Duotone tool icons

The owner's review supersedes the initial all-sourced selection: use the approved
OLD column as the base and replace a glyph only when the open-source version is
clearly more recognisable. The final 94-ID family restores **88 approved colour
and mono pairs exactly** and keeps **six reviewed source replacements**. All six
have filled family material, dark borders and restrained highlights. Toolbar
layout, tool behavior, caches, preferences and settings remain unchanged.
Changes remain uncommitted on `wip/codex-icons-sourced`.

## Selection and restored metaphors

The approved colour snapshot is `docs/design/tool-icons-pre-sourced.json`, which
supplies the comparison sheet's OLD column. The matching approved mono bytes are
preserved in `docs/design/tool-icons-approved-mono.json`. Both originate at Local
Image commit `2b3989c0`. `docs/design/tool-icons-before.json` is an older palette
reference, not the owner's selected comparison baseline.

Sourced improvements retained: **Crop, Rectangle Marquee, Ellipse Marquee, Eraser,
Gradient and Brush**. The other 88 use the approved originals. In particular:

- Dodge: filled lollipop/paddle head with a handle; no candy spiral.
- Count: numbered marker; no tally marks.
- Burn: filled cupped hand; no twisted outline hand.
- Forward Warp: filled finger pushing; no four-way move arrows.
- Content-Aware Move: filled green directional glyph in its selection frame.
- Blur: filled green droplet; the other outline-only regressions are restored too.

No new substitute was selected for the remaining tools: the approved metaphor
already satisfies the owner's decision. There are no sourced variant badges.
No proprietary artwork was downloaded, traced or copied.

other IDs. It preserves their exact paths, coordinates, fill rules and transforms.
Paint is deliberately adapted to the owner's requested material: opaque family
and neutral-metal gradients, dark outlines, and highlights capped at 28%.

opaque coloured center. Crop's two native open paths are broad material bands,

highlight. Source-specific paint widths are recorded in the manifest. Native
viewBoxes normalise to 24×24 with the approved 1.5-unit padding and 0.875 scale.

SVGs use explicit white paint so egui can apply the foreground tint, including
on dark themes. The style test now checks this white base as well as equal alpha
at 16/20/40 px. The restored Dodge test again requires a filled paddle head.

opaque center and substantial coloured material in the other five replacements.
The generator test checks complete coverage, freshness, pins, unchanged native
geometry and the absence of unused source files in the manifest.

renderer. Sources followed: prior builder's Glossy Duotone material in

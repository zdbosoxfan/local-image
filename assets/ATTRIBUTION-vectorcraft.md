# VectorCraft

Local Image adapts vector appearance and live-group data designs from [VectorCraft](https://github.com/storytold/vectorcraft), pinned to `d522c1d7be4035bd4f4a84cd6ebfca44f5155092`.

Copyright (c) 2026 ArtCraft Team and the VectorCraft contributors. MIT OR Apache-2.0; see `licenses/vectorcraft-LICENSE-MIT`, `licenses/vectorcraft-LICENSE-APACHE`, and `licenses/vectorcraft-NOTICE`. Adaptations use Local Image layers, Fill/ShapeStroke and native persistence. No VectorCraft renderer, text stack or brand assets are included. Individual adapted files are recorded in `docs/PORTS.md`.

    About Quick Heal

    Texture repair copies nearby texture to cover small objects. Dust & scratches smooths tiny defects. Both run locally without a model download.

    Texture repair: Embark Studios texture-synthesis (MIT / Apache 2.0). Dust & scratches: OpenCV Telea inpainting (Apache 2.0).

    Overwrite original image?

    This will overwrite your original image.

    Use Save a copy to save a separate copy instead.

    Close image?

    Closing clears the working layers.

    Save an editable .lremove project to keep the original and layers. PNG, JPEG, TIFF, and WebP saves are flattened images.

    Pending brush and pen selections are not included in a project and will be cleared when you close.

Quick Heal

Texture repair copies suitable nearby texture to cover small objects. Dust & scratches uses a smoothing fill for tiny defects. Both run locally on the CPU, without a model download. Use AI Remove for larger objects or complex scene reconstruction.

Texture repair: Embark Studios texture-synthesis (MIT / Apache 2.0).

Dust & scratches: OpenCV’s Telea inpainting (Alexandru Telea, 2004).
OpenCV is licensed under Apache 2.0.

Qwen texture cleanup: DeGrid (Apache 2.0).
